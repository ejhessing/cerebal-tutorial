Help & Guide

Goal

Find and reach the exit (yellow flag) with at least 1 survivor before winter kills your colony.

Controls

  • Left Click - Select hex / Build
  • Right Click - Pan camera
  • Scroll - Zoom in/out
  • WASD / Arrows - Move camera
  • R - Reset camera view

Seasons

  • Spring: Normal production
  • Summer: +1 food bonus
  • Fall: 20% animal attacks
  • Winter: -50% food, need wood for heat

Building Tips

  • Build on matching terrain for bonuses
  • Tier 2 unlocks at 5 buildings
  • Tier 3 unlocks at 10 buildings
  • Build heaters before year 2
  • Scout towers reveal 2-hex radius

Important

  • Winter needs wood for heating
  • Winters get harder each year
  • Resources deplete to 50% production
